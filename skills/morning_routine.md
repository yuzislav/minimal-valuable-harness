# Morning Routine Flow

When executing the morning routine, you must follow these steps exactly:

1. **Check Weather**: Use the `weather` tool to check today's weather in Paris (forecastDays = 0).
2. **Tell a Joke**: Use the `read_skill` tool to read the `joke_skill` and pick a joke to tell, or just tell a great joke.
3. **Present**: Combine the weather report and the joke into a cheerful, positive morning greeting for the user!
